# Export source review and restart recovery

## OpenShot

- `OpenShot/libopenshot` at `e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa` keeps separate size-limited working and finished caches. `FFmpegReader::GetFrame` looks for a finished picture first, continues decoding for nearby forward requests, and seeks backward to an earlier timestamp for distant or backward requests. `Seek` clears working state, flushes both decoders after the seek, and reopens the file if the seek fails. `CheckSeek` retries earlier when the landing overshoots. `IsPartialFrame` prevents earliest post-seek frames from entering the finished cache. VlogCraft can apply the separation between transient preroll and verified decoded pictures in `native_project_plan.py` and the Phase 2 export worker. OpenShot applies a frame-count margin before its frame-to-PTS conversion and uses frame-based landing and retry checks. VlogCraft's camera path needs exact source ticks and codec-specific random-access evidence for VFR, B-frames, and HEVC leading pictures. [Cache and forward reuse](https://github.com/OpenShot/libopenshot/blob/e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa/src/FFmpegReader.cpp#L1339-L1407) · [Landing checks](https://github.com/OpenShot/libopenshot/blob/e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa/src/FFmpegReader.cpp#L1994-L2056) · [Seek and flush](https://github.com/OpenShot/libopenshot/blob/e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa/src/FFmpegReader.cpp#L2624-L2763) · [Partial-frame rejection](https://github.com/OpenShot/libopenshot/blob/e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa/src/FFmpegReader.cpp#L3009-L3023).
- At the same revision, `FFmpegWriter` feeds a single encoder context through successive `WriteFrame` calls, drains delayed audio and video packets, interleaves timestamped packets, and writes the trailer at close. That maps to VlogCraft's continuous x265 stream, bounded renderer-to-encoder delivery, final audio graph and atomic publication in `native_project_render.py` and `server.py`. Its generic writer does not establish VlogCraft's Main10 PQ, mastering-display, HDR10+ picture-association or exact PCM requirements. [Frame submission](https://github.com/OpenShot/libopenshot/blob/e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa/src/FFmpegWriter.cpp#L1149-L1163) · [Close and drain](https://github.com/OpenShot/libopenshot/blob/e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa/src/FFmpegWriter.cpp#L1247-L1357) · [Encoder ingress and interleaving](https://github.com/OpenShot/libopenshot/blob/e6924a3c8668f5786e6fca0024a6394fabacfaaa/src/FFmpegWriter.cpp#L3054-L3155).
- `OpenShot/openshot-qt` at `f168c512c0e724a4dbfdff04bdd3b1c8938d70e4` launches an offscreen Qt worker from a serialized project and export specification. `export_worker.py` stages into a job-specific partial path, samples progress every 15 frames into an atomically replaced status document and checks for cancellation between frames. It closes the writer before replacing the final path. Its shutdown cleanup removes unfinished output, and this file contains no restart recovery of completed spans. VlogCraft adopts the immutable snapshot and atomic status publication in `export_jobs.py`, with a process identity and explicit reconciliation after a crash. Its existing scratch lease and final publication rules remain the authority for media files. [Export worker](https://github.com/OpenShot/openshot-qt/blob/f168c512c0e724a4dbfdff04bdd3b1c8938d70e4/src/classes/agent/export_worker.py#L40-L108).

## MLT and Kdenlive

- `mltframework/mlt` at `e7bdca6914335d0ba033d1292f115292accd78de` has distinct producer and consumer implementations. `producer_avformat` identifies an initial timestamp, samples packet durations for VFR, continues for nearby requests, and uses a backward demuxer seek for discontinuities. It flushes the decoder, drops queued packets from the old position, and maps reordered decoded-frame timestamps back to the requested position. VlogCraft can adopt decoder reuse, seek invalidation and presentation-ordered selection. MLT derives its seek target from nominal frame rate, uses a configurable cutoff, and guesses zero for certain negative DTS cases. Those heuristics do not prove the exact original picture selected at a VFR cut or open-GOP HEVC landing. [Producer seek](https://github.com/mltframework/mlt/blob/e7bdca6914335d0ba033d1292f115292accd78de/src/modules/avformat/producer_avformat.c#L1793-L1965) · [Reordered picture position](https://github.com/mltframework/mlt/blob/e7bdca6914335d0ba033d1292f115292accd78de/src/modules/avformat/producer_avformat.c#L3130-L3168).
- At the same revision, `consumer_avformat` pulls frames on its own thread, feeds continuous audio and video encoder contexts, interleaves encoded packets, and drains and closes the container. Its stop path requests shutdown and joins the consumer. VlogCraft's D3D11 producer and continuous x265 consumer can remain independent under bounded backpressure, with frame order and the final audio endpoint verified at the delivery boundary. The MLT source demonstrates producer/consumer lifecycles. It supplies no verified D3D11-to-x265 Main10 transfer or cross-segment HDR10+ contract. [Stop and join](https://github.com/mltframework/mlt/blob/e7bdca6914335d0ba033d1292f115292accd78de/src/modules/avformat/consumer_avformat.c#L706-L738) · [Frame acquisition](https://github.com/mltframework/mlt/blob/e7bdca6914335d0ba033d1292f115292accd78de/src/modules/avformat/consumer_avformat.c#L2878-L2944) · [Drain and trailer](https://github.com/mltframework/mlt/blob/e7bdca6914335d0ba033d1292f115292accd78de/src/modules/avformat/consumer_avformat.c#L3059-L3151).
- `KDE/kdenlive` at `e4675c0802adcf97b99067cde8f7e1c4cf6a7314` connects `QProcess` output and exit events to `RenderJob`, parses monotonic frame and percentage progress, and reports terminal status back to the editor over a local socket. Cancellation kills the owned process and removes its destination. The completion path checks process exit and output existence before notification. VlogCraft adopts supervision and terminal reporting principles. It keeps its existing destination on failure and verifies delivery separately. A socket terminal message or a file's existence alone cannot establish completed delivery after an unclean exit. [Cancellation and progress](https://github.com/KDE/kdenlive/blob/e4675c0802adcf97b99067cde8f7e1c4cf6a7314/renderer/renderjob.cpp#L100-L215) · [Completion and output checks](https://github.com/KDE/kdenlive/blob/e4675c0802adcf97b99067cde8f7e1c4cf6a7314/renderer/renderjob.cpp#L267-L350).

The inspected libopenshot files declare LGPL-3.0-or-later, the OpenShot Qt worker GPL-3.0-or-later, the MLT avformat files LGPL-2.1-or-later, and Kdenlive's `renderjob.cpp` GPL-3.0-only OR LicenseRef-KDE-Accepted-GPL. VlogCraft's implementation uses the architectural ideas and its own code.

## FreeCut

`walterlow/freecut` at `5ceeeb388eef5423640b7db157706274bf1b8165` uses an OPFS-backed Mediabunny `StreamTarget` to deliver encoded output in bounded chunks. The resulting `File` backs the returned `Blob`. An in-memory `BufferTarget` serves browsers without working OPFS. Failed work can abort the writable stream, and the browser work area has age-based cleanup. VlogCraft already stages native encoded output to disk. The applicable idea is to keep bulk encoded bytes on disk and bound staging memory on the long-export path. At startup, VlogCraft verifies owner identity when determining whether a job was interrupted and leaves scratch intact for verification. FreeCut's browser implementation and MIT license are documented upstream. [File-backed target](https://github.com/walterlow/freecut/blob/5ceeeb388eef5423640b7db157706274bf1b8165/src/runtime/renderer/export-output-target.ts#L34-L139) · [License](https://github.com/walterlow/freecut/blob/5ceeeb388eef5423640b7db157706274bf1b8165/LICENSE).

## VlogCraft startup reconciliation

Before launching work from the Export or review-video entry point, the editor atomically saves the frozen export project and a job receipt with its SHA-256 in the project workspace. The receipt stores the destination, launch options, editor PID and process creation time. Progress writes are limited to one per five seconds when progress callbacks occur. Terminal transitions flush immediately. On startup, both desktop and local server check for an exited or recycled owner PID, reread the receipt to preserve a terminal result written immediately before exit, and atomically mark a job that is still recorded as running as interrupted. Access-denied owner checks remain indeterminate. The Export dialog can show the latest interrupted job and its last recorded progress.

Reconciliation touches receipt metadata only and leaves source files, snapshots, scratch and the destination intact. A destination may predate the interrupted run, or a host may exit after publication but before recording completion. Both cases retain their existing file and receive an interrupted status when a terminal receipt is absent. The previous job is never relaunched on startup. An unreadable receipt is retained for inspection. Existing confirmation-gated scratch cleanup remains available for app-owned artifacts.

The 3,489-picture legacy job was launched by a separate local QA supervisor. Its status document has `supervisorPid`, `workerPid`, `stage`, `status` and wall-clock timestamps. It replaces the status file atomically after stage changes and relies on separate activity and project files. It has no process-creation identities or verification receipt for the aborted master. The normal editor journal is a separate format. The legacy status, project, media and scratch were inspected read-only, and the legacy full-export process was never relaunched.

## Segment checkpoint evaluation

A future segment manifest should bind the following fields before a reusable segment can be admitted.

| Field | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Project and sources | Frozen project SHA-256, resolved source stream IDs, source content hashes and clock/index identity for every dependency, including stills and effects assets. File size, identity and modification time can accelerate invalidation but cannot authenticate content. |
| Output interval | Absolute half-open frame interval `[first, end)` on the rational project clock, exact source-picture IDs for the span, and dependency bounds for any cross-cut or temporal effects. |
| Encoder and color | FFmpeg/x265 and filter versions, complete effective encoder options, deterministic GOP policy, dimensions and timebase, bit depth and chroma, Rec.2020/PQ range and matrix, and the exact mastering-display and light-level declarations. |
| Artifact and verification | Owned path, byte length and SHA-256, picture count and exact PTS/duration, independent decoder and boundary checks, color and static HDR checks, and dated verification results. |
| Completion | File close, successful verification and atomic manifest publication timestamps. |

Reuse would require matching the current frozen spec and source identities, a verified manifest, and a matching file hash. Crash recovery would retain completed verified segments and take a new claim under the existing scratch ownership model. Only the missing spans would rerender. Large source and segment hashes can run on bounded workers and should be accounted for in export wall time. Any incompatible or unverifiable segment requires a fresh render of its dependency span or the continuous reference path.

Independent x265 segment encodes reset lookahead, rate control, reference pictures and GOP decisions. Each candidate splice needs an independent random-access picture, a closed dependency boundary and compatible parameter sets. Assembly must preserve exact PTS, DTS and durations across boundaries and tile the whole output clock. A continuous audio graph should generate audio once against that clock. VlogCraft can author HDR10+ from the assembled final decoded pictures and verify per-picture metadata associations after assembly. Qualification must include boundary and global decoded PQ PSNR against the continuous reference, exact PCM endpoints and timing, and all static/dynamic HDR declarations. Segment checkpoint reuse remains a design pending that qualification. The interrupted MP4 cannot supply an arbitrary append position.
